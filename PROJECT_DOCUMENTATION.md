# SevaSetu | Quick Project Overview (Short Summary)

A 3-in-1 platform for citizen government services (Aadhaar, PAN, GST, ITR), local retailer Kendra operations, and platform admin management.

---

### 🌐 1. Citizen Website (Public Portal)
* **Zero Public Pricing**: Prices differ across cities/regions, so rates are completely hidden from public view.
* **Inquire Button on Every Service**: Every service card features an **"Inquire for Price & Booking"** button to request city-specific quotes and doorstep assistance.
* **Automatic Admin Notification**: When a citizen submits an inquiry, it **instantly notifies and appears in the Admin Portal** in real-time.
* **Service & Document Directory**: Clear turnaround times and document checklists with **PDF / Image** format badges and **Mandatory** tags.
* **Live Status Tracking**: 4-step progress tracker (*Submitted ➔ Assigned ➔ Verification ➔ Completed*).
* **Company & Contact**: Support desk, UIDAI/NSDL compliance standards, and instant inquiry logging.

---

### 🏪 2. Retailer Panel (Kendra Operations)
* **Dashboard**: Live wallet balance, today's & lifetime commissions, recent transactions, and assigned customer leads.
* **Assigned Services**: Shows Citizen Price vs Kendra Cost vs Guaranteed Commission (e.g., GST: ₹700 profit, PAN: ₹70 profit).
* **"Apply for Citizen"**: Fill customer details, verify document checklist (`[x]`), attach PDF/Image, auto-debit wallet, auto-credit commission, and print receipt.
* **Instant Wallet Recharge**: Top-up via simulated UPI QR (GPay/PhonePe) or NetBanking with instant balance update.
* **Ledger & Profile**: Commission breakdown by service, transaction history vouchers, and bank/KYC details.

---

### 🛡️ 3. Super Admin Portal (Governance)
* **Secure Admin Login**: Dedicated login with email, password, and 4-digit Master Security PIN (`8821`), plus 1-click access.
* **Executive Dashboard**: Platform-wide metrics, wallet in circulation, and 1-click **"Approve Retailer"** alert.
* **Retailer Management**: Add, approve, reject, activate/deactivate, and view Kendra dossiers.
* **Category Management**: Create, edit, and delete service domains.
* **Service Management ("What Needed" Documents)**:
  * Manual service entry (Name, Category, Citizen Price, Retailer Margin, Status).
  * **Document Builder**: Title Name, `[x] PDF` checkbox, `[x] Image` checkbox, and `[x] Mandatory` checkbox.
* **Inquiries Inbox**: Route website customer leads to nearest local Retailer Kendras.
* **Wallet Settlement Desk**: System-wide ledger and manual credit/debit adjustment tool.
